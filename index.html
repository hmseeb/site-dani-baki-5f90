Three ways we work with you

Each one starts the same way — a short conversation about what you need, then a written quote so you can decide with the numbers in front of you.

01

Know where you stand before you spend anything

Consultation and assessment

Book an appointment and we'll go through the job with you properly: what's involved, what it will take, and the options you actually have. You finish the conversation with a written scope and a price you can compare, not a vague ballpark.

  • Appointment at a time you choose and we confirm
  • Clear scope written down, in plain language
  • Options explained, including doing less for now

02

Get the job done once, properly

Scheduled project work

Once the quote is agreed, the work is booked into a confirmed slot and carried out to the scope you signed off. If anything changes along the way, you hear about it from us before it costs you money.

  • Fixed scope agreed in advance
  • Confirmed appointment, not a loose window
  • No change to the price without your say-so

03

Keep it working without chasing anyone

Ongoing support and maintenance

For anything that needs looking after over time, we set a simple routine: agreed visits, agreed checks, agreed cost. You know who to contact and you get an answer when you do.

  • Visits planned ahead with you
  • One point of contact for questions
  • Quoted per visit or per period — your choice
